For ITMD 521, Big Data Technologies

## Objectives

* Video record your learnings from the lab

## Outcomes

* At the completion of this lab, you will have demonstrated the ability to run PySpark applications on a remote cluster. You will be able to use version control to bring your code to a remote system, you will be able to demonstrate reading and storing datasets in Object Storage. Finally you will be able to explain the process you used to filter records out of the dataset that do not meet specified levels for Air Temperature.
